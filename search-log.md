## Screening

- Records fetched with full metadata: 300 unique PubMed identifiers (stored in `src/pubmed_metadata.json`).
- Abstracts read in full after title screening: 151.
- Full text opened for five research letters without abstracts, including Choi 2026 (JAMA Network Open, US Veterans) and Silverii 2024 (69 trials).
- Excluded: narrative reviews without new data, animal and laboratory studies (except one mechanism study cited), case reports (except those cited as examples), studies of fewer than about 100 people per group, duplicate reports of the same cohort.
- Extraction notes with every figure and DOI: `src/notes.md`. Study rows as drawn on the page: `src/data_rows.py`; built data: `src/data.json`.

## Not found or not available

- Results of the FOCUS trial (semaglutide and diabetic retinopathy, NCT03811561): no publication in PubMed as of 28 October 2026.
- Full text of the research letters Fung 2025 (older people with diabetes), Goldenberg 2025 (letter meta-analysis) and Abboud 2026 (NAION with optic disc drusen).
- Any cohort measuring NAION risk on GLP-1 drugs by optic disc shape, any study of AMD measured on retinal imaging, and any New Zealand data.
- Direct access to the NCBI website was blocked by the network proxy; all PubMed work used the connector.
